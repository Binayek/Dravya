// Studio Identity

Matter → Form → Space

Origin of the Name

Dravya (Sanskrit: द्रव्य) signifies elemental substance, foundational matter, or material reality. We believe space cannot exist without an honest understanding of the physical matter from which it is formed.

Connecting Thinking with Making

Dravya was established to eliminate the disconnect between architectural drawing boards and construction sites. Rather than producing paper designs handed off to external contractors, we operate as a unified architectural and construction entity.

Every project begins with a deep study of local context—seismic stability, natural lighting angles, regional soil bearing capacities, and local timber and stone reserves.

✦ Physical Authenticity

We do not hide materials behind synthetic coatings. Unadorned stone, structural timber, raw concrete, and lime plasters are celebrated.

✦ Single Responsibility

By integrating design with construction execution, we eliminate client budget overruns, design compromises, and contractor disputes.

Client Journey (01 → 08)

// Practical Client Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on fees, project durations, municipal approvals, and design-build logistics.

What is the difference between Design-Only and Design + Build?

▼
In a Design-Only engagement, Dravya acts purely as the architect, delivering full blueprint sets, structural drawings, and material schedules for you to tender out to independent third-party contractors.

In a Design + Build engagement, Dravya takes complete single-point responsibility for both designing the building and managing the physical construction, craftsmen, material procurement, and quality control on site.

How are professional fees structured?

▼
Architectural design services are typically calculated as a fixed rate per square foot or a percentage (usually 5% - 8%) of the total estimated construction cost depending on scope complexity.

For Design + Build construction, costs are broken down into an itemized, transparent Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed material and labor milestone payments.

Does Dravya handle municipal permits and building approvals?

▼
Yes. We prepare complete structural calculations, floor plans, and elevation documentation aligned strictly with the Nepal National Building Code (NBC) and local municipal ward requirements for official building permit approval.

What service areas does Dravya cover?

▼
Our primary studio operations and construction teams are based in Pokhara and Kathmandu. However, we accept select architectural design and master planning commissions throughout Nepal.

What is the typical project duration from design to completion?

▼
• Architectural Design & Municipal Approval Phase: 6 to 10 Weeks.
• Residential Construction Execution: 8 to 14 Months (depending on area scale and weather seasons).
